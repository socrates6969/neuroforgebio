# ADR 0010: Order of external assurance spend

- Status: **Accepted**
- Date: 2026-09-26
- Deciders: owner (Marius Carlsson) at GATE B
- Source: `architecture/DECISIONS.md` D10, `BRIEF.md` "GATE B decision". Company name: NeuroForge Bio.

## Decision

1) Privacy counsel reviews the jurisdiction RuleSets before the first paying ledger customer. 2) A regulatory consultant reviews the FDA Evidence Kit before it is sold. 3) SOC 2 Type II starts once 2-3 design partners ask; until a report exists the website says "on roadmap".

## Consequences

Agents never contact counsel or consultants or spend money; quotes are owner actions. Copy-lint bans compliance claims such as "certified" or "SOC 2 compliant".
