# Blueprint Assumption Validation

Verdicts: **SUPPORTED** / **WEAK** (partial, indirect, or untested) / **CONTRADICTED**. Sources are those opened on 2026-09-26 (see landscape.md, regulation.md and sizing.md for full citations and grades).

| # | Blueprint assumption | Verdict | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Startups spend months rebuilding pipelines"** | **WEAK** (plausible, not measured) | *For:* the EEG-BCI systematic review (Peksa 2026, *Sensors*, A) found code/pipeline availability in only **20.9%** of 129 papers, with heterogeneity in acquisition, layouts, tasks and preprocessing. *Sci Adv* 2026 (A): "inconsistent data formats and variable data quality". *Against or unknown:* no source quantified startup engineering time. Free building blocks are heavily used: MNE 308k downloads/month, BrainFlow, LSL, braindecode | Run 15–20 discovery interviews with engineering leads before building. Measure weeks spent on ingest, sync and formats. Do not put "months" in marketing copy until measured |
| 2 | **"Compliance is the moat"** | **WEAK for HIPAA; SUPPORTED for neural-specific privacy** | HIPAA infrastructure is a commodity: AWS "175+" HIPAA-eligible services incl. Timestream/SageMaker; Aptible HIPAA at $499/mo; W&B HIPAA on Enterprise. HIPAA often **does not apply** to consumer or research neurotech (it covers only covered entities and business associates). By contrast, 4 state laws with **conflicting definitions** (CO/CA: central + peripheral; CT: CNS only; CA excludes inferred data), the MIND Act FTC-study bill (Sep 2025), and EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) create real, specialised work. No neural-specific compliance tool was found | Re-frame the moat as **neural-data governance** (classification, consent, deletion lineage, use restrictions), not "HIPAA + AES-256" |
| 3 | **"Free academic tier → seed-stage spinouts"** | **WEAK** | This is exactly NeuroPype's model: academic free, startup $79–99/mo for entities with ≤$200K revenue or raised, enterprise by quote. No evidence was found on conversion rates. Academics already have **free, publicly funded** platforms: DANDI (2,208 users; NIH BRAIN), OpenNeuro (1,902 datasets), brainlife ("publicly funded by NSF, DoD, Kavli, NIH"; 2,000+ users). CatalystNeuro already serves 69 labs with NWB conversion | Keep a free tier as a **distribution channel** (GitHub integrations with MNE/BIDS/NWB/DANDI), not a revenue engine. Target spinouts directly |
| 4 | **"BCI hardware makers are the core buyer"** | **CONTRADICTED for leaders; WEAK for the long tail** | Leaders build in-house: Synchron's "Chiral" foundation model plus NVIDIA Holoscan and a native Apple integration; Neuralink is vertically integrated. The well-funded set is about 7 companies. Non-invasive vendors sell their own SaaS (EmotivPRO $0/$89/$224.08 per month) | Primary buyers become **clinical-stage device startups needing FDA evidence** and **CNS pharma/CROs**. Hardware makers are secondary |
| 5 | **"Automated neuro-cleaning (ICA, filtering, artifact rejection) is a value driver"** | **CONTRADICTED as a one-size default** | Kessler 2025 (*Commun Biol*, A): **all artifact-correction steps reduced decoding performance**, and higher high-pass cutoffs increased it. Huang 2025 (*Psychophysiology*, A): 43 pipelines, "no single best pipeline" | Sell **provenance + sensitivity analysis** (pipeline multiverse) rather than "auto-clean". Record every preprocessing parameter |
| 6 | **"Neuro-AI model marketplace (motor intent, cognitive state)"** | **WEAK** | Open substitutes are free and used: braindecode (22k/month), pyRiemann (64k/month). Outputs driving a clinical BCI are likely **device software** (FDA device-software policy; implanted-BCI guidance requires an Enhanced Documentation Level). EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) bans emotion inference in workplace and education. No evidence of demand for a paid marketplace | Defer. If built, make it a **governed model registry** with use-restriction, provenance and SOUP documentation, not a consumer-style app store |
| 7 | **"Low-latency time-series storage" as a differentiator** | **WEAK** | Commodity TSDBs are HIPAA-eligible (Amazon Timestream on AWS's list). LSL handles real-time LAN streaming for free | Build on commodity storage. Differentiate on **verified end-to-end latency measurement** instead (see new-ideas #3) |
| 8 | **"Neural-data privacy laws (Colorado, Connecticut, Montana …) create demand"** | **SUPPORTED** | CO HB24-1058 (effective 7 Aug 2024), CA SB 1223 (Ch. 887, 28 Sep 2024), MT SB 163 (May 2025, via peer-reviewed review), CT PA 25-113 (signed 24 Jun 2025; CTDPA sections effective 1 Jul 2026). More states are considering bills. MIND Act introduced Sep 2025 | Lead with this. The CT effective date (1 Jul 2026) has already passed, so buyers are exposed now |
| 9 | **"Investor/market momentum supports a neurotech infra company"** | **SUPPORTED (sector), WEAK (for infra)** | Neurotech device VC was $1.2B in 2024 ("more than any other type of medical device", *Sci Adv* 2026 citing HSBC). HSBC 1H26: "Neuro remained the strongest medtech investment category". But the dollars go to hardware/clinical companies; no sourced infra-software rounds were found | Pitch as "the evidence and governance layer for a $1B+/yr funded sector", not as a BCI company |
| 10 | **"SDKs in Python, C++, Unity/Unreal are needed"** | **WEAK** | Python demand is proven by the download stats. C++ is covered by liblsl and BrainFlow (multi-language). No evidence was found for Unity/Unreal demand; Neurosity and Emotiv already ship SDKs | Python first; C/C++ via LSL/BrainFlow interop; Unity/Unreal only on a customer pull |

3. Is CatalystNeuro-style conversion a business worth entering, or a partner channel?
