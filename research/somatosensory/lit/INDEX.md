## Evidence base in one paragraph
About 12 people worldwide have had S1 ICMS arrays across 4 groups (Pitt/Chicago, Caltech, JHU, Case Western). Findings are
consistent across groups for somatotopic contact percepts, graded intensity, thresholds in the tens of uA, and multi-year
projected-field stability. Most other claims come from n=1-5 studies at one consortium (grade B). No human studies were found
on ICMS texture discrimination or controllable thermal percepts. Continuous proprioceptive feedback has not been shown in humans.

## Key sources (all opened, audited)
- Flesher et al. 2021 Science, PMID 34016775: ICMS feedback halved ARAT trial time (20.9 -> 10.2 s, n=1).
- Greenspon et al. 2025 Nat Biomed Eng, PMID 39643730: JND 13.5 uA median; biomimetic multi-electrode 19.5 discriminable levels vs 8.
- Greenspon et al. 2026 Sci Transl Med, PMID 42455900 (+ preprint 40832410): 5 people, up to 10 y, no serious AEs; ~62-64% electrodes functional at end.
- Hughes et al. 2021 eLife (34313221), 2021 JNE (34320481), 2022 Brain Stim (35671947): frequency effects, thresholds over time, adaptation.
- Hobbs et al. 2025 (40106898); Valle et al. 2025 (39818881): biomimetic naturalness; edges, motion and shapes via spatial patterning.
- Callier et al. 2019 (30668644): onset/offset transients dominate S1 responses. Saal et al. 2017 (28652360): TouchSim.
- Weber et al. 2013 (24082087): millisecond-precise texture code. Vestergaard et al. 2023 (36755097): warmth is represented in posterior insula, not in S1.
