## 0. What this document is (read first)

**This is a POST-HOC redesign.** It was written AFTER the author saw the N1 and N2 results (results\N1_verdict.json,
results\N2_results.json, code\RESULTS_N1_N2.md) and the independent review (code\REVIEW_N1_N2.md, code\review\*). It is **not blind**.
N1 part (b) FAILED. The review showed that the failure came from mis-specified controls in the prereg, not from a code bug.
This document replaces **only** N1(b). It does not edit N1 or N2. Those files stay as they are, and their hashes (27db9d27..., ed929ae0...)
must still match at run time.

The results of N1 (a), (c), (d) and (e) stand as run and as confirmed by the review. The name **N1'** means those four parts plus N1b.
N1' is PASS only if (a), (c), (d), (e) (already PASS) and N1b are all PASS.

Why a post-hoc gate can still be informative, and what it cannot do:
- Each control below has a null distribution that is **exact by construction**. It is known before any run, because the test labels the
  control is judged against are generated by us from a known sampler (§2).
- Each control must also **trip on a planted leak** (§4). So a control that is too weak to fail cannot produce a PASS.
- What remains is a researcher degree of freedom in *choosing* this design after seeing the old one fail. §6 states how that
  limits the status of N2.

## 2. NC-P: phantom-seizure negative control (replaces NC1 and NC2)

### 2.1 Ictal exclusion
For every real seizure [on, off) in every file (train and test), the interval [on - 600 s, off + 900 s) (clipped to the file) is EXCLUDED.
What remains are **allowed segments**: contiguous intervals inside one file. The allowed time is computed from notes\data\chbmit_selection.csv
by the author before writing this:

| subject | train allowed h (segments) | test allowed h (segments) |
|---|---|---|
| chb01 | 5.81 (9) | 2.11 (6) |
| chb03 | 1.81 (4) | 6.27 (12) |
| chb10 | 4.90 (4) | 6.27 (8) |

Each allowed segment is given to the UNCHANGED pipeline as a pseudo-record. It keeps its absolute start time, so the F4 buffer and the F6 causal guards
still apply. Its windows are built inside it, and it is scored as its own recording, as files are in N2 §3. The inner LOFO folds group
pseudo-records by their original training file. Windows in excluded time are never fitted, normalised, OOF-scored or test-scored.
Real seizures are therefore never negatives. The 900-s post-ictal margin is a pragmatic choice: 30 min would leave chb01 almost no test time.
Validity does not depend on this margin (§2.3). Only cleanliness and power do.

### 2.2 Phantom labels, injected at the label vault
- Per subject and replicate r, draw **training phantoms**: one per real training seizure, with the same durations (chb01 40/27/40 s; chb03
  52/65/69 s; chb10 35/70/65 s). Use stream k = 10.
- Separately, draw **test phantoms**: one per real test seizure, with the same durations (chb01 51/90/93/101; chb03 52/47/64/53; chb10 58/76/89/54).
  Use stream k = 11.
- **Sampler S (this is the definition).** The phantoms are placed in a fixed order: longest first, ties broken by file order.
  - Each phantom's onset is drawn uniformly over all (segment, integer onset) pairs that satisfy two conditions:
    - [onset - 30, onset + d + 60) lies inside the segment.
    - The gap is >= 300 s to every phantom already placed in the same segment.
  - Draws are made by exact enumeration of the valid set, not by rejection. If the valid set is empty, the replicate is redrawn and
    the count is logged. The author predicts that no redraws will be needed.
- The phantom 1-Hz masks are loaded into a `PhantomVault` that exposes the same interface as the real vault.
  - The harness reads training labels (`vault.train_mask`) and test labels (only through `Scorer`) along its normal code paths.
  - Any path in the harness that could leak test labels into training would therefore leak **phantom** test labels. That is what NC-P detects.
- **Order is enforced.** The final model's test scores s and the hypothesis masks h are computed first, and their SHA-256 is written to the
  N1b log. Only then is stream 11 used to draw the test phantoms. The log shows this order.
- Real test annotations are used only to build the exclusion (§2.1), and that exclusion is independent of the phantoms.

### 2.3 Why the null is exact
- Under an honest harness, s and h are functions of the training data, the training phantoms and the test EEG only.
- The test phantoms are drawn from S, independently of all of these.
- For any statistic T(s or h, test labels), draw M = 999 fresh placements from the same sampler S (stream k = 12). The randomisation p-value is
  p_up = (1 + #{T_null >= T_obs}) / (M + 1). This is exactly valid (super-uniform, because of ties) for every replicate, whatever the EEG, the score
  autocorrelation or any residual peri-ictal state.
- The lower-tail p_lo is defined the same way with <=.
- The replicates are independent: new training and test draws each time. So under the null, their p-values are independent and super-uniform.
- The expected value of T under the null is E_S[T | s], computed from the same M draws. No band has to be assumed.

### 2.4 Statistics, the degenerate-model rule, and the criteria
R = 10 replicates per subject, 30 in total (the only fallback is R = 5, under the budget rule in §7).
- **T_A (window arm):** test window AUROC of the raw p_i against the phantom window labels (N2 window-label rule), on allowed windows only.
- **T_E (event arm):** per-subject event F1 against the phantom references (TP, FP and N_ref summed over pseudo-records, N_ref = 4).
  - The hypothesis uses the **rate-matched tau** t_rm. This is the smallest grid tau at which the training-OOF hypothesis has at most 1.323 scored events per hour of allowed
    training time. The target rate is the honest N2 model's test rate (26 scored events / 19.655 h, from review c3_pooled_poisson).
  - The events are counted after the 90-s merge and 300-s split, **without labels**, so t_rm is label-free.
  - If no grid tau qualifies, t_rm = 0.99 and the replicate is flagged.
- **Locked-rule arm (descriptive, NOT in the gate):** T_E is also computed at the N2 tau* chosen on training OOF against the training phantoms. Report tau*
  and the degeneracy flags.
- **Degenerate replicate (label-free, decided from h before the test phantoms are drawn):**
  - ALWAYS-ALARM: h covers >= 50% of the allowed test seconds.
  - NEVER-ALARM: 0 hypothesis events on the test.
  - Degenerate replicates are REPORTED and EXCLUDED from the T_E Fisher combination. This is valid because the exclusion depends on h only.
  - AUROC is kept, unless the SD of s is < 1e-6, in which case the replicate is flagged and excluded from T_A.
- **Combination:** Fisher X = -2 sum ln p over the retained replicates, referred to chi2 with 2n degrees of freedom. This is done separately for (T_A, up), (T_A, lo),
  (T_E, up) and (T_E, lo).

**NC-P validity: PASS iff all of the following hold:**
- V1. All 4 Fisher p-values are >= 0.0025. By the union bound, the family false-FAIL rate is <= 0.01 under an honest harness.
  - Upper tail: evidence that the harness leaks test labels.
  - Lower tail: a sign or indexing error, or the old NC2-type anti-learning.
- V2. At most 10 of the 30 replicates are degenerate in the event arm, AND at most 3 of 30 are flagged in the window arm. Otherwise the control is
  **powerless**: N1b cannot PASS.

## 3. C3': random-alarm null with an exact reference (replaces C3)

Inputs: the N2 primary hypothesis masks (unchanged code, recomputed; §6) and the real test references, 12 seizures in 16 test files.

- **Generator (as in D8).** Each file's 1-Hz hypothesis mask is circularly shifted by an independent offset, uniform on {0, ..., T_f - 1}. This keeps
  the alarm count, the durations and the rate exactly.
- **Exact distribution.**
  - For each file, enumerate all T_f offsets with the re-implementation scorer (validated in N1(e)). This gives the exact pmf of (TP_f, FP_f).
  - Convolve over the files to get the exact pmf of the pooled (TP, FP). Map it to pooled F1 = 2TP / (2TP + FP + (12 - TP)), with NaN -> 0.
  - Record the mean mu*, SD sigma*, 95th percentile q95 and exceedance mass a* = P_exact(F1 > q95) <= 0.05.
- **Harness Monte Carlo.** M_C = 1,000 draws (stream k = 14), scored through the harness `Scorer` (timescoring).
- **PASS iff both hold:**
  - (i) |mean_MC - mu*| <= 3 sigma* / sqrt(M_C).
  - (ii) The fraction of MC draws with F1 > q95 is <= a* + 3 sqrt(a*(1 - a*)/M_C). For a* = 0.05 that is 0.0707.
- **Descriptive only.** The N2 model's pooled F1 (0.632) is compared with q99. This contrasts the model with chance and is not part of the gate.

## 4. Planted leaks: each control must trip (power)

Every planted leak runs through the same N1b code, behind an explicit `allow_planted_leak=True` flag. The same call without the flag must
raise. This is a new pytest test, in the style of S5.

| leak | mechanism | must trip | trip rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| PL-A label leak (C2a-type, strong) | 25% of the test windows on allowed time (random, stream k = 13), with their PHANTOM labels, are added to the training set and to the normaliser fit. They remain in the test scoring. t_rm and the degeneracy rule are as in NC-P. R = 5 per subject. | NC-P T_A and T_E | Fisher (up) p < 0.0025 for T_A AND for T_E |
| PL-B threshold leak (F3-type) | On the 30 NC-P fits: tau is chosen to maximise the test event F1 against the TEST phantoms (the F3 tag check is bypassed on purpose). No refit. | NC-P T_E | Fisher (up) p < 0.0025 for T_E. Specificity check: the T_A p-values must be identical to NC-P's, because the scores are unchanged. |
| PL-C scorer/alarm leak | In the C3' generator, for each seizure-containing test file with probability 0.25 (stream k = 15), the offset is replaced by one that makes the first alarm event overlap the real seizure. 1,000 draws. | C3' | the fraction of draws with F1 > q95 is > a* + 3 sqrt(a*(1 - a*)/M_C) (the (ii) bound) |

- If any trip rule fails, N1b = **FAIL (controls powerless)**, whatever the validity results.
- A label-free leak (normaliser fitted on test, C2b) carries no label information, so NC-P cannot see it and is not required to.
  That leak is covered by the F1 guard and its violation test in N1(d).

## 5. Order of work and the synthetic dry run

1. **Tests.** pytest on code\tests\ plus the new N1b tests (flag guard; sampler S enumerates the valid set exactly on a hand fixture; order
   log). Everything must pass.
2. **Synthetic dry run** (stream k = 16; the existing synthetic EDF generator; 3 synthetic subjects with the real file and seizure layout).
   - NC-P uses 100 replicates.
   - The pooled p_up values of T_A must pass a one-sample KS test against U(0,1) with p >= 0.01 (discreteness is ignored; M = 999).
   - PL-A must trip.
   - A failure here is a CODE bug: fix it, log it in DEVIATIONS.md, and it does not count as a fix round on real data.
3. **Real run.** Hash check of N1, N2, N1b and all code; snapshot DEVIATIONS.md into results\ (review note 5(i)); N2 identity recompute (§6);
   NC-P; PL-A; PL-B; C3'; PL-C.
4. **Rerun.** A second fresh-process run must give a byte-identical N1b card (sorted keys, repr floats, runtime block excluded), as in N1(c)3.

N1b is **PASS** iff steps 1-4 hold, NC-P V1-V2 hold, C3' (i)-(ii) hold, and every trip rule in §4 holds.

## 6. How N2 is re-evaluated

**Rule.**
- If N1' is PASS, the N2 numbers that were already computed are reported under the **unchanged** N2 §4 decision rule:
  - chb01 INCONCLUSIVE (4/4, FP 5).
  - chb03 INCONCLUSIVE (4/4, FP 9).
  - chb10 PASS (4/4, FP 0).
  - Overall **INCONCLUSIVE**.
- If N1b FAILS, N2 stays "not verifiable: code". Under N2 §8 that is final for chb01/03/10.

**Is this legitimate, or must N2 be re-run? It is legitimate, with conditions and a label. No new N2 run is needed. What is required is a byte-identity check that the old numbers are the ones the locked code produces.**
- **Why it is legitimate.**
  - The N2 statistic depends only on objects that were locked and hashed before any test label was read: the split, the model, the tau rule, the scorer, the decision grid and the code (review §1 and §5).
  - N1 is a validity gate on the code. It is not an input to any N2 number.
  - Redesigning the gate cannot move chb01's FP count or any other N2 value. Re-running N2 "as-is" would, by the determinism shown in N1(c), reproduce the
    same bytes. So a full re-run adds no information beyond that identity check.
  - Seeing the test labels matters only where a degree of freedom could still act on the estimate. No such freedom touches N2.
- **Why a condition and a label are still needed.**
  - The degree of freedom that remains is on the gate itself: a gate designed after seeing results could be chosen so that it passes.
  - This is limited by three things:
    - the exact nulls (§2.3, §3), which contain no tuned constants;
    - the mandatory planted-leak trips (§4);
    - the independent code review, which already found no test-to-train path.
  - It is not eliminated. So the N2 verdict is labelled **"N2 locked-rule verdict; harness gate re-validated post hoc (N1b)"**. It is never labelled plain "confirmatory",
    and N2 §6's blind predictions are reported next to it, misses included.
  - This partly disagrees with the review's "exploratory" label: the numbers are not exploratory in the sense of having been chosen after the fact, but the gate is post hoc.
    The queen decides the wording in the report. Both views are recorded here.
- **Identity check (mandatory, part of the N1b run).**
  - The nfharness/*.py, edf_reader.py and run_n2.py SHA-256 values must equal those in results\N2_run_card.json.
  - The N1b run recomputes the N2 primary (all 3 subjects) with that unchanged code.
  - tau*, TP, FP, N_ref, H and window AUROC (repr) must equal results\N2_results.json byte for byte.
  - Any difference means N2 = "not verifiable: code". S-a and S-b are not re-run; they stay descriptive.
- The chb01, chb03 and chb10 test files remain **burned** for any new model (N2 §8).

## 7. Budget, seeds, abandonment

- **Budget:** < 15 min CPU and < 1.5 GB peak RAM per real run (the rerun has its own budget), BLAS threads = 1, seed 20261001.
  - Streams: numpy.random.default_rng(SeedSequence(20261001, spawn_key=(k, subject_index, replicate))) with k = 10 training phantoms, 11 test phantoms, 12 null draws,
    13 PL-A selection, 14 C3' MC, 15 PL-C, 16 synthetic dry run.
  - Estimate: features 26 s; NC-P 30 fits with LOFO about 200 s; PL-A 15 fits about 100 s; null draws (rank-based AUROC, event re-implementation) about 60 s; C3' exact
    enumeration (about 55k offset scorings) about 60 s; MC 1,000 with timescoring about 120 s; N2 identity about 40 s. Total about 10 min.
  - Pre-approved fallbacks, logged, in this order: M_C = 500; R = 5; R_leak = 3. Nothing else. Exceeding the budget after the fallbacks stops the run.
- **Abandonment:** one fix round for CODE bugs found on real data (logged). If N1b fails for a DESIGN reason, there is no N1c on these subjects. N2 stays
  "not verifiable: code", and harness validation moves to N3 with fresh subjects under a blind prereg.
