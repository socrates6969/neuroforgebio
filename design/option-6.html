How it works

From electrode to insight, in four gentle steps.

We take care of the messy middle — formats, noise, versioning, models — so your people can spend their time on the science.

  1. Step 01

    Ingest

    Hardware-agnostic connectors for EEG, ECoG, and microelectrode arrays, landing in low-latency time-series storage.

    • BIDS
    • LSL
    • EDF/BDF
    • NWB
    • XDF
  2. Step 02

    Clean

    Automated neuro-cleaning — filtering, ICA, artifact rejection, re-referencing, normalization — as versioned, reproducible pipelines with provenance.

  3. Step 03

    Neuro-AI

    A model marketplace for motor-intent decoding and cognitive-state estimation — or bring your own model. Benchmarks published with methods.

  4. Step 04

    SDKs

    Python, C++, Unity and Unreal. Stream in, get predictions out — in the tools your team already loves.

    • Python
    • C++
    • Unity
    • Unreal

Four lines to a clean signal.

Connect a stream, run a cleaning pipeline you can cite, and call a model — the same way on a laptop, in the cloud, or at the edge.

Read the docs 
import neuroforge as nf
stream = nf.connect("lsl://headset-01")
clean  = stream.pipeline("ica-default").run()
intent = nf.models.get("motor-intent/v1").predict(clean)

Security & compliance

Trust, by design.

Brain data is some of the most personal data there is. Here's how we plan to look after it — in plain language, with honest labels on what's live and what's on the roadmap.

By design

Encrypted everywhere

AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Your recordings are locked whether they're sitting still or on the move.

By design

The right people, the right data

Role-based access control and audit logs, so you always know who touched what, and when.

BAA planned

HIPAA-aligned workflows

Built to support HIPAA-aligned workflows. A Business Associate Agreement for enterprise customers is planned.

Roadmap

SOC 2 Type II

An independent audit of our controls is on the roadmap. We'll say so clearly when it's done — not before.

By design

De-identification tooling

Strip identifying metadata before data is shared with collaborators or used to train models.

Planned

Data residency

Options to keep data in the region your institution or regulator requires are planned.

Neural-data privacy, taken seriously. We're building to support emerging US state neural-data privacy laws — consent, access, and deletion controls included. (This is product design intent, not legal advice.)
