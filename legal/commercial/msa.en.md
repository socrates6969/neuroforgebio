## 1. Definitions
- **Service**: NeuroForge's hosted platform described in the Order Form (e.g. consent & deletion ledger, provenance graph, pipelines, model registry) and its web console and APIs. Status at signature: features are **designed/planned** unless the Order Form lists them as generally available.
- **Beta Features**: features marked beta, preview, early access or similar. Excluded from the SLA and warranties (§ 9.3).
- **Customer Data**: all data submitted to the Service by or for Customer, including neural signals, recordings, metadata, consent records, pipeline definitions and trained model weights produced by Customer.
- **Outputs**: results generated by the Service from Customer Data at Customer's instruction (e.g. processed datasets, derived features, trained models, deletion certificates, provenance exports).
- **Aggregated Service Metrics**: technical usage and performance data about the operation of the Service (e.g. API call counts, job durations, error rates, storage volumes) that contain **no Customer Data content** and do not identify Customer, any user or any data subject.
- **SDK**: NeuroForge software development kits. Open-source parts are licensed under Apache-2.0; other parts under the Platform/Client Licence (sdk-licence.en.md).
- **Personal Data, Controller, Processor, Special Categories**: as in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).
- **Neural Data**: data generated by measuring activity of the central or peripheral nervous system, including as defined in applicable US state laws.
- **Fees**: the fees in the Order Form. **Subscription Term**: as in the Order Form.

Consent-scope checks in the Service (e.g. "model training", "commercial use" scopes in the consent ledger) do not replace this opt-in.
4.3a **Research pool (optional, PLANNED; default OFF).** Customer may choose to contribute De-identified copies of selected datasets to NeuroForge's research pool by signing the **Research Pool Addendum** (research-pool-addendum.en.md), which is the written opt-in under § 4.3. 4.6 **Optional AI assistant ("ask your data") – PLANNED.** (a) The assistant is optional per tenant, **OFF by default**, and can be enabled only by a tenant administrator. Customer may disable it at any time. (b) When enabled, it sends only metadata and provenance (study/dataset descriptors, pipeline and provenance-graph information, channel classifications) and the user's question to a third-party AI model provider, currently planned to be Anthropic (a sub-processor under DPA Annex III). Customer must verify outputs before relying on them and must not use them for clinical decisions (§ 10.1). The assistant is excluded from the SLA and the § 9.1 warranty while it is Beta.
