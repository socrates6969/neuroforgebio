# Memo: "ask your data" AI assistant using the Anthropic API

To: owner (Marius Carlsson), nfb-legal queen · From: nfb-legal-commercial · Date: 2026-09-26 · Status: DRAFT. Feature status: **PLANNED**. ASSUMPTION: NeuroForge Bio will be a Norwegian AS, not yet incorporated.

**Owner decision (given):** an optional assistant calls the Anthropic API. It sends only metadata and provenance: study/dataset descriptors, pipeline and provenance-graph information, and channel classifications. It never sends raw neural signals or direct identifiers.

## 1. Bottom line
1. **Is it personal data? Usually yes.** Metadata tied to a subject pseudonym is personal data (Recital 26). It stays personal data even if we strip the pseudonym but a study-level field still lets someone single out a subject. Study-level descriptors with no subject rows (e.g. "EEG, 40 participants, motor imagery") are probably **not** personal data about an individual.
2. **Does it trigger Art. 9? It can.** If what we send reveals a health condition of pseudonymous subjects, it is "data concerning health". Examples: an "ALS cohort" study title sent together with subject-level provenance nodes, or per-subject clinical fields. That is special-category data, whether or not a raw signal is included.
   - **Design rule:** send study-level metadata without subject-level rows, and redact diagnosis-revealing titles and clinical fields. Then Art. 9 is normally avoided.
   - Where it is not avoided, the customer's existing Art. 9(2) condition for the study covers our processing as its processor. That still needs the Anthropic sub-processor authorisation and an updated DPIA.
3. **US state neural-data laws.** The metadata we send is, on a plain reading, **not "neural data"** in CO, CA or CT, because it is not information "generated by measuring" nervous-system activity. **Derived signal features** (band power, spike rates, decoded intent) likely **are**, so they stay off the allow-list. MT: statute not opened – **UNVERIFIED**.
4. **Contract route:** Anthropic Ireland, Limited as a **planned sub-processor**, under general written authorisation with advance notice and a right to object (DPA § 6, Annex III). The transfer runs on SCC Module Three via Anthropic's DPA. Do not rely on the EU–US DPF (Anthropic's documents do not mention it). Request ZDR.
5. **AI Act:** NeuroForge is the **provider** of the assistant (an AI system built on a third-party general-purpose AI model, placed on the market under our name). The customer is a **deployer**. Anthropic is the GPAI model provider. The practical duty is transparency: tell users they are interacting with AI (Art. 50(1), applicable from 2 Aug 2026). We must also never build Art. 5 practices into it. It is not high-risk as designed (no Annex III use). Keep the assistant out of clinical and employment decisions.

## 2. Personal data and Art. 9 (GDPR text opened, publications.europa.eu)
- **Art. 4(1):** personal data is information relating to an identifiable person, identifiable "directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as … an online identifier or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental … identity".
- **Art. 4(5) and Recital 26:** "Personal data which have undergone pseudonymisation, which could be attributed to a natural person by the use of additional information should be considered to be information on an identifiable natural person." Identifiability accounts for "all the means reasonably likely to be used, such as singling out".
  - The customer holds the key to its subject pseudonyms. Our provenance graph is keyed on those pseudonyms.
  - Any **subject-level** node (e.g. `sub-017 → recording → epoching run`) is therefore personal data, even in NeuroForge's and Anthropic's hands.
  - Small cohorts plus rare conditions raise singling-out risk.
- **Art. 9(1)** prohibits processing of "data concerning health" and of "biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person", unless Art. 9(2) applies. **Art. 4(15):** "data concerning health" is personal data related to physical or mental health "which reveal information about his or her health status".
  - A study title like "ALS cohort", combined with subject-level nodes, reveals each subject's diagnosis, so it is health data.
  - So are per-subject clinical fields (diagnosis, medication, UPDRS score).
  - Channel classifications ("C3, EEG, central nervous system, CO: neural") reveal nothing about health on their own.
  - Biometric identification is not the assistant's purpose. The assistant must never be asked to match or identify people (AUP § 1).
- **Conclusion:** use the design rule in § 1.2. Treat any residual subject-level payload as Art. 9 data covered by the customer's study basis (Art. 9(2)(a) explicit consent or (j) research). Record this in the DPIA.

## 4. Sub-processor authorisation (Art. 28(2), (4) – text opened)
- **Art. 28(2):** a processor needs "prior specific or general written authorisation of the controller". Under general authorisation it must "inform the controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of other processors, thereby giving the controller the opportunity to object".
  - Our DPA § 6 uses general authorisation with **[30] days' notice** and a right to object and terminate.
  - **Action:** list Anthropic in Annex III now, marked PLANNED (done). Before first activation, send the § 6.2 notice to every customer whose DPA predates the listing.
- **Art. 28(4):** the same data-protection obligations must flow down to Anthropic "by way of a contract".
  - Anthropic's DPA (effective 24 Feb 2025) is a processor DPA: 48 h breach notice, a 15-day objection period for its own sub-processors, deletion within 30 days after termination, and SCC Modules 2/3.

## 6. Safeguards (required before launch)
1. **Allow-list of fields sent** (default deny): dataset/study ID (tokenised), modality, channel counts and classifications, sampling rates, pipeline/step names and versions, run status, provenance edge types, consent-scope *names* (not contents), jurisdiction labels, and aggregate counts ≥ [k=10] (ESTIMATE).
2. **Server-side redaction:** subject IDs are replaced with per-request ephemeral tokens (or dropped). Strip or never send: free-text notes, clinical/phenotype fields, study titles and descriptions unless the tenant marks them "non-sensitive", file paths containing names, and dates of birth or sessions [or shift them]. Signal arrays and derived signal features are blocked at the serializer (unit-tested).
3. **Per-tenant opt-in:** OFF by default and enabled only by a tenant admin, with an in-product notice naming Anthropic. Per-user "AI" indicator (AI Act Art. 50(1)). A kill switch per tenant and globally.
4. **Logging:** audit-log every assistant call: user, tenant, field list sent (not content), and the model/version. Keep prompts and outputs out of application logs (Schedule 2 S6).
5. **Retention and training:** request **ZDR** from Anthropic. Until ZDR is contracted, disclose the 30-day retention and the Usage-Policy-flag exception (up to 2 years). Training on API data is contractually excluded ("may not train models on Customer Content").
6. **DPIA update** (RISK-MEMO § 9): add the assistant as a processing operation. Assess singling-out risk, Art. 9 residuals, the transfer (TIA) and output accuracy.
7. **Customer notice:** a DPA Annex III update and a § 6.2 notice ≥ [30] days before first activation, plus release notes. The MSA § 4.6 disclaimer covers accuracy and says outputs are not medical or regulatory advice.
8. **Use limits:** the system prompt and UI state that the assistant must not identify people, give clinical advice, or infer emotions (AUP § 2). Refuse and log such requests.
9. **Model/entity changes:** any switch of AI provider triggers the sub-processor notice procedure.

## Kort norsk sammendrag
KI-assistenten sender bare metadata og proveniens til Anthropic (Anthropic Ireland, Limited for EØS-kunder). Den er av som standard og slås på per leietaker.
- **Personopplysninger:** metadata knyttet til pseudonymer er personopplysninger (fortalepunkt 26, art. 4 nr. 1 og 5).
- **Art. 9:** avslører metadata en diagnose (f.eks. «ALS-kohort» sammen med data per person) eller inneholder kliniske felt, er det helseopplysninger etter art. 9. Derfor sender vi bare studienivådata og sladder kliniske felt, titler som avslører diagnose, og person-ID-er.
- **Delstatslover:** i Colorado, California og Connecticut er slik metadata etter en naturlig lesning ikke «neural data», men avledede signaltrekk er det trolig. Montana er **UVERIFISERT**.
- **Anthropic:** Anthropic legges inn som planlagt underdatabehandler med varsel og innsigelsesrett. Overføring skjer etter SCC modul 3, ikke DPF. Anthropic trener ikke på kundedata, og standard lagring er 30 dager. Vi ber om nullagring (ZDR).
- **KI-forordningen:** NeuroForge er tilbyder; kunden er idriftsetter. Brukerne skal få vite at de snakker med KI (art. 50 nr. 1).
- **Neste steg:** DPIA-en må oppdateres, og kundene må varsles før assistenten tas i bruk.
