# Terms for frittstående tegningsretter (warrants), asl §11-12

**ASSUMPTION:** [NeuroForge Bio AS] will be a Norwegian AS under aksjeloven. It is not yet incorporated; all company data
are placeholders. **Language:** the Norwegian version (tegningsretter.no.md) prevails.
**Warning:** warrants create hidden dilution for the owner (STRUCTURES.md §4(c): 2.7–4.4 pp at the seed). Use only if required, and keep them
small and short-dated.

## 1. Formal requirements (verified from asl §11-12, via the fetch-tool summary)
The general meeting resolves, with the majority required to amend the articles, to issue tegningsretter. The resolution
**must** state:
1. the number of warrants to be issued;
2. who may subscribe for the warrants;
3. the deadline for subscribing for the warrants (at least two weeks where pre-emption applies);
4. the consideration for the warrants;
5. the conditions for demanding shares, including the consideration for the shares;
6. the deadline for exercising the right to demand shares, **not later than five years from the resolution**;
7. the share class, if relevant;
8. the warrant holders' rights on capital changes, reorganisations or dissolution;
9. from when the new shares carry dividend rights.
Pre-emption: §§10-4 and 10-5 apply correspondingly (§11-13). Registration: reported to Foretaksregisteret under §11-6
via §11-13. [Advokat: check whether §11-12 limits the total nominal amount, and whether the board can be authorised to
issue warrants; **UNVERIFIED**.]

## 2. Terms (template)
These terms are identical to term-sheet-warrant.en.md, tegningsretter.no.md and STRUCTURES.md §4(c), and follow
investor\ROUND-ASSUMPTIONS.md (c): shares at NOK 8M pre-money + a warrant for up to NOK 1M at a NOK 20M pre-money cap,
24–36 months.

2.1 Amount and number (item 1): the warrants give the right to subscribe new shares for **up to NOK [1,000,000]** in
total. A **maximum** of [___] warrants is issued (= NOK 1,000,000 ÷ a floor price of NOK [___], at least the nominal
value NOK [___]). Each warrant gives the right to 1 new share. Only as many warrants as NOK 1,000,000 buys at the
exercise price may be exercised; the rest lapse. [Advokat: confirm that a maximum number satisfies §11-12 item 1.]
2.2 Holder (item 2): [Investor]. Subscription for the warrants takes place in the GF minutes by [date].
2.3 Consideration for the warrants (item 4): NOK [0].
2.4 Exercise price (item 5): the **lower** of (a) the price per share in the next priced equity round (seed) and (b) NOK
[20,000,000] ÷ the number of shares outstanding immediately before the seed (excluding the 10 % option pool created
pre-money in the seed). It is never lower than the floor price and is payable in cash.
2.5 Exercise period (item 6): only in connection with the closing of the seed. The right lapses at the earlier of (a) the
closing of the seed and (b) [24–36] months after the GF resolution; in any case **not later than five years** after the
resolution. Unexercised warrants lapse without compensation.
2.6 Exercise: written notice to the board + payment. The board resolves the capital increase and reports it for
registration [advokat: exact procedure].
2.7 Adjustments (item 8): on share splits or bonus issues, the number and price are adjusted so the economic value is
unchanged. There is no adjustment for issues at market value. On a merger, demerger or dissolution, the holder may
exercise before the effective date [advokat].
2.8 Dividend rights (item 9): from registration of the new shares.
2.9 Share class (item 7): ordinary shares.
2.10 Transfer: not transferable without the Company's consent.
