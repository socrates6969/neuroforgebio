through a directed share issue (rettet emisjon). This is a pre-seed round.

## 2. The investment
2.1 The Investor shall subscribe for [antall] new shares, each with nominal value NOK [___], at a subscription price of
NOK [___] per share (total NOK [1,000,000]). This reflects a pre-money valuation of NOK [10,000,000] (fully diluted; no option pool exists before this round). After the issue, the Investor holds [9.09] % and the Founder [90.91] % (ESTIMATE; investor\ROUND-ASSUMPTIONS.md).

asl §10-7 [advokat to confirm the form].

## 3. Conditions precedent
Closing is conditional on: (a) the Company being registered in Foretaksregisteret; (b) the Company's warranties (clause
5) being true at closing; (c) the shareholders' agreement being signed; (d) the general meeting resolution being passed;
(e) [other].

## 4. Closing and payment
4.1 The Investor pays the subscription amount to the Company's separate share-contribution account [kontonr.] within
[__] days after the resolution (asl §§10-12 and 10-13 on payment and the separate account, **advokat to check details**).
4.2 The Company obtains a confirmation that the contribution has been paid from a revisor or, for cash, from a
finansforetak, an advokat or a statsautorisert regnskapsfører (asl §10-9), and reports the capital increase to
Foretaksregisteret within three months of the end of the subscription period.
4.3 The new shares carry full rights from registration (asl §10-11 **UNVERIFIED detail**).

## 5. Company and Founder warranties (limited, owner-friendly)
As of signing and closing: (a) the Company is duly incorporated, and the share capital and shareholder register are as
set out in Appendix 2; (b) no other rights to shares exist (options, convertibles, warrants) except as disclosed;
(c) the Company owns or has the right to use its source code and IP, and the Founder has assigned all IP he created for
the Company's business to the Company [Appendix 3 IP assignment]; (d) no litigation is pending; (e) no data
processing that the Company knows to be unlawful. **Status statements on the product use "designed / planned"
wording; the product is pre-product.**

## 6. Limitation of liability
6.1 Claims must be notified within [12] months of closing.
6.2 Total liability of the Company and Founder is capped at [the subscription amount] [owner-friendly: 50 %]. There is
no liability for losses under NOK [50,000] each. The Founder is liable only for wilful misconduct or fraud [negotiable].
6.3 Compensation may, at the Company's choice, be paid in new shares at the subscription price [advokat to check the
feasibility under asl §10-2].

## 7. Use of proceeds; pro-rata right
7.1 Product development, hiring and operations under the Company's budget (about 8–18 months of runway depending on team size; ESTIMATE, investor\ROUND-ASSUMPTIONS.md).
7.2 The Investor has a pro-rata right in the next equity round (seed) only, as set out in the shareholders' agreement
clause 5.2. A 10 % option pool will be created pre-money at the seed and will dilute the Founder and the Investor pro rata.
