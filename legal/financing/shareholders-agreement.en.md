# Shareholders' agreement: [NeuroForge Bio AS]

**ASSUMPTION:** the company will be a Norwegian AS under aksjeloven. It is not yet incorporated; all company data are
placeholders. **Language:** the Norwegian version (aksjonaeravtale.no.md) prevails.
**Important:** a shareholders' agreement binds only its parties. Where a clause departs from aksjeloven's default rules
or from the articles (e.g. forkjøpsrett, consent to transfer, board composition), the **articles (vedtekter) must be
amended to match** by a general meeting resolution under asl §5-18 (see generalforsamlingsprotokoll, part D).
Otherwise the company law rule prevails towards the company. [Advokat: list which clauses need matching articles.]

Parties: Marius Carlsson (the "Founder"), [Investor] (the "Investor"), [NeuroForge Bio AS] (the "Company"), and
any later shareholder by deed of adherence (Appendix A).

## 1. Purpose and precedence
1.1 The agreement governs the relationship between the shareholders.
1.2 If this agreement and the articles conflict, the parties shall vote to amend the articles to match this agreement,
to the extent aksjeloven allows.

## 2. Board, observer and general meeting
2.1 Board: [1–3] members. The Founder nominates [all / all but one]. [Alternative: the Investor nominates one member
while it holds ≥ [8] %.] **Owner-friendly default: the Investor has an observer right** (attends meetings and receives
papers, no vote, bound by confidentiality).
2.2 General meetings follow aksjeloven; majority requirements under asl §§5-17 to 5-20.

## 3. Reserved matters
While the Investor holds ≥ [5] %, the following require its written consent (short list, negotiable): (a) change of
business purpose; (b) issue of shares or rights to shares at a price per share below the price in the [pre-seed] round,
other than the 10 % employee option pool created pre-money at the seed round (investor\ROUND-ASSUMPTIONS.md); (c) dividends or other
distributions to the Founder or related parties outside market terms; (d) winding-up or a merger in which the Company is
not the surviving company, **except in a Drag-along sale (clause 7)**.

## 4. Information rights
Quarterly: short report (revenue, cash, burn, runway, key events). Annually: accounts within [5] months of year-end and
next year's budget. Rights lapse below [3] %.

## 5. Pre-emption in new issues; pro-rata right
5.1 Shareholders have pre-emption rights in new issues under asl §10-4. The parties may agree to waive it by GF
resolution (§10-5).
5.2 **Pro-rata right:** in the next equity round (seed) only, the Investor may subscribe for up to its pro-rata share on
the same terms, within the seed's fixed size. The right lapses after that round. The parties undertake to vote for a GF resolution that makes this
possible.

## 6. Transfer of shares: consent and forkjøpsrett (right of first refusal)
6.1 No transfer of shares for [12] months after closing without the other party's consent, except to wholly owned
holding companies (the transferee adheres).
6.2 **Forkjøpsrett:** on a transfer, the other shareholders may buy the shares pro rata on the same terms. Deadlines:
[30] days from notice. [Advokat: align with the vedtekter or exclude the statutory forkjøpsrett in the vedtekter and
rely on this clause. asl §4-15(3) makes forkjøpsrett the default unless excluded in the vedtekter.]
6.3 Forkjøpsrett does not apply to Tag-along or Drag-along sales.

## 7. Drag-along (medsalgsplikt)
If shareholders holding ≥ [2/3] [owner-friendly: > 50 %] of the shares accept a bona fide offer from an independent third
party for all shares, all other shareholders shall sell on the same terms and price per share. There is no personal
warranty by the Investor beyond title and capacity. Liability is several, not joint, and pro rata.

## 8. Tag-along (medsalgsrett)
If the Founder wants to sell shares so that a third party obtains > 50 %, the Investor may sell the same proportion of its
shares on the same terms.

## 9. Founder commitment, and vesting through a call option (UNUSUAL; NEGOTIABLE)
9.1 The Founder works full time for the Company and does not compete for [12] months after leaving [advokat: check
reasonableness and compensation rules for non-compete; **UNVERIFIED**].
9.2 Vesting for a sole founder-owner is **unusual**. Investors sometimes ask for it to protect against the founder
leaving. **Owner-friendly default: no vesting.** If the Investor insists, implement it as a **call option** for the
Company (or the other shareholders) over "unvested" founder shares, not as a transfer restriction in the vedtekter:
- Vesting: [25] % vested at closing (reflecting prior work); the rest vests monthly over [36] months; **full
  acceleration** on a sale of the Company or termination without cause.
- **Good leaver** (death, illness, termination without cause, leaving after [12] months): unvested shares may be
  bought at **market value**.
- **Bad leaver** (resigning within [12] months, material breach, dismissal for cause): unvested shares may be bought at
  **the lower of nominal value and market value** [owner-friendly: at [50] % of market value].
- The option is exercisable within [3] months of leaving. The Founder always keeps vested shares.

## 10. Confidentiality and non-solicitation
Standard confidentiality. The Investor does not solicit the Company's employees for [12] months.

## 11. Duration and termination
The agreement ends for a party that no longer holds shares, and in full on a listing or a sale of 100 % of the shares.
