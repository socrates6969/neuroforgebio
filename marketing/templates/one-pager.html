Research and development software. Pre-product: every capability is designed, planned or on the roadmap. Not a medical device; no diagnostic or therapeutic claims.

The problem

  • Pipelines change results. Artifact-correction steps reduced decoding performance (Kessler et al., Commun Biol 2025). 43 pipelines, "no single best pipeline" (Huang et al., Psychophysiology 2025).
  • Pipelines are rarely shared. Code or pipeline availability in 20.9% of 129 EEG-BCI papers (Peksa et al., Sensors 2026).
  • Neural data is regulated, inconsistently. Colorado, California and Connecticut (effective 1 Jul 2026) treat neural data as sensitive data with different definitions, and Montana adopted SB 163 in 2025 (per a peer-reviewed review; statute text not yet reviewed); EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) bans emotion inference in workplaces and education except for medical or safety reasons. Not legal advice.
  • Regulated devices need documentation. FDA's implanted-BCI guidance points to the Enhanced Documentation Level for device software.
