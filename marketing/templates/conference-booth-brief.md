## 3. Demo script (5 minutes, laptop or screen; public or synthetic data only)
> **No live EEG or other neural recording of attendees.** Recording visitors would itself create neural data subject to the laws we talk about, and would need consent processes we do not run. Use a public DANDI/OpenNeuro dataset (check its licence) or clearly labelled synthetic data.

1. **Hook (30 s):** "Two 2025 studies found preprocessing choices change decoding results, and no single pipeline was best. How does your team choose?" (Kessler 2025; Huang 2025.)
2. **Provenance (1 min):** load a public dataset with the open-source tool (only if released; otherwise show designs labelled "design, not product"). Show every parameter recorded.
3. **Multiverse (1.5 min):** run [N] pipelines side by side and show how the metric shifts. Label numbers "demo data, public dataset [NAME]".
4. **Governance (1.5 min):** show the planned ledger design: one recording classified differently under CO, CA and CT definitions. Say "planned" and "not legal advice".
5. **Ask (30 s):** "Would you be up for a 30-minute call about how you handle this today?" Offer the one-pager.

- **Badge scanning**: only if the visitor agrees verbally for that scan; import only the fields above.
- **Storage**: owner-controlled storage only; no third-party enrichment or tracking tools.
- **Retention**: delete non-engaged leads after [12] months; delete on request immediately.
- **No neural data**, no photos of visitors without consent.
- Counsel to confirm GDPR applicability for an EU venue (Croatia). Not legal advice.
